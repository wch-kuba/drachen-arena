# Part 12 – Progress & problem words

**Goal:** the parent sees in 10 seconds what is going well and what to practice.

← [Back to plan](../PLAN.md)

## Tasks
- [ ] Overview: rounds played this week, minutes, crystals, per-exercise accuracy (first-try %).
- [ ] Simple trend per exercise (last 4 weeks).
- [ ] **Problem words list:** sorted by wrong count / stuck in box 1, showing what the child actually typed (e.g. "Hauten" for Hüte).
- [ ] Mistake patterns: "Umlaut im Plural vergessen: 6×", "ü statt i: 3×".
- [ ] Action per word: "mehr üben" (push to front) or "ausblenden".
- [ ] Optional: share a summary (screenshot-friendly view) with the teacher or the other parent.

## Done when
The parent can name the 3 words the child struggles with most, straight from this screen.
